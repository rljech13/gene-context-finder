<details class="panel block"><summary>Methods and limits</summary><div class="content"><p>Candidate identity and coding-model quality are separate assessments. “Consistent candidate” means that the saved target-reference measurements and source naming agree under the prototype rules; it is not a confirmed orthology assignment. The prototype requires at least 50% coverage of both proteins for this label, without imposing a universal sequence-identity threshold.</p><p>Gene trees are computed independently for each comparison group. Identical full proteins share one leaf with all aliases retained. Columns with less than 50% canonical-residue occupancy are excluded by default; the exact mask is downloadable. Source alignments retain all columns. Branch support is UFBoot support, not a probability that a gene name is correct. No target-versus-paralog clade classification or duplication/loss history is inferred automatically.</p><p>Structures must match the exact full protein sequence. TM-scores are normalized separately to each retained structure; coverage is relative to each original full protein. “Confident residues” means pLDDT ≥70, not a domain annotation. Missing structures are not evidence of a wrong model. Similar structures do not distinguish every paralog or validate biological function.</p><p>Tables use zero-based, half-open coordinates. Displayed loci are one-based inclusive. No expression or real-mRNA claim is inferred from sequence or structure prediction.</p><p><a href="evidence.json">Evidence JSON</a> · <a href="manifest.json">Run manifest</a> · <a href="seal.json">Checksums</a> · <a href="finalization.decisions.json">Input final choices</a> · <a href="structure_comparisons.tsv">Structural measurements</a></p></div></details>
